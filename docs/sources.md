# Source register (W7)

Owner: Saif (G). Last checked: Nov 10, 2026.

Every baseline figure used in the cost model, the memo or the report is listed here with where it came from and whether we confirmed it. Status is one of: confirmed (seen in a City or primary document), corrected (our earlier number was wrong or mislabelled), unconfirmed (could not find a primary source, do not use).

## Outcome of the task 9 check

* The 1.58M interactions figure for 2025 is real. It appears in a 2026 Customer Experience Division presentation to Executive Committee. We can now use 2025 as the volume year.
* The 537,000 service requests figure for 2025 is still unconfirmed. No City document we found states a 2025 service request total. Use the confirmed 2024 figure, or state the 2025 number as an estimate (see S4).
* The handle time conflict is resolved. 312 seconds is the 2024 actual. The 4 min 52 s in our research summary (292 s) matches the 2023 actual of 293 s, so it was an older year, not a different measure. Use 312 s.
* New and important: the City pays 311 representatives $35.17 to $38.53 an hour, far above the Job Bank regional median of $20.30 the proposal used. The $35 to $55 fully loaded range in the proposal is too low. See assumptions.md.
* New: Toronto launched a public AI Assistant on Sept 28, 2026. It answers general questions only and cannot take service requests, which confirms our scope choice (intake, not general inquiries).

## Notes for the report

* Mixing years: the budget is 2026, the handle time is 2024 and the best volume is 2025. Say so wherever a ratio combines them.
* "Up 5.7% from 2024" on the S2 slide sits under the satisfaction figure, not the volume. Do not quote it as volume growth. The jump from 1.29M to 1.58M is about 22% and probably reflects new channels and integrations (email service requests from July 2025, Parks from Sept 2025), so the two years may not be counted the same way.
* Toronto scale for the cost model: about 463,000 service requests a year is roughly 39,000 a month. Our 1M a month scenario is about 26 times Toronto and should be described as a large multi city or provincial scale, not Toronto.
* Open item: S16 needs one look at the PDF itself. S5 stays out of the report unless someone finds the 2025 annual report.
