# Waste collection in Gilead (synthetic)

Gilead collects garbage, recycling and organics at the curb. Recycling and organics are collected every week. Garbage and yard waste are collected every other week, alternating.

Carts must be at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection day, with the lid closed and at least 1 metre between carts. Carts placed out after the truck has passed are not considered missed collections.

Residents can report a missed collection through 311. The report needs the address, which stream was missed (garbage, recycling, organics or yard waste) and the scheduled collection day.

Damaged or missing carts are replaced by Solid Waste. A report needs the address, the cart type and what is wrong with it (for example a cracked lid, a broken wheel, or a stolen cart).

Yard waste goes in paper yard-waste bags or open containers, not in plastic bags. Branches must be bundled, under 1.2 metres long and under 7.5 cm in diameter.
